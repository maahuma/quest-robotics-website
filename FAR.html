Meet Team Borobots

Our team at Quest Robotics knows exactly what it's like to struggle for robotics funding and resources, because we come from the same situation. Based in Freehold Borough High School, FTC Team #30730, Borobots, spent our rookie season struggling. Coming from a Title 1 school, we grew frustrated with our lack of funding and resources for robotics, losing almost an entire season to fundraising and playing catch-up. Despite all this, we received the Sustain Award in our conference. Now, we want to change this situation not only for Team Borobots, but for other youth in the Freehold/Freehold Borough area.

It's personal

With a passionate group of robotics high school students backing us up, the Freehold Area Robotics division of Quest will be a tight-knit community of student builders, whether they want to compete or just build.
