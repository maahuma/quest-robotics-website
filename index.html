We are a student-led organization, dedicated to improving access to  Robotics Education  for youth through materials, guidance, and community.

Driven by our own experiences with struggling to bring robotics education to our school/community, we aim to improve  3  things:

Funding & Logistics

Whether you're based in a Title 1/underfunded area or are just starting out, funding is one of the biggest hurdles for youth trying to break into robotics. Part of our Quest is to ease the logistical stress on educators and youth so they can get started. We help groups get access to funding and manage startup logistics, so that they don't have to spend months fundraising when kids could be building.

Mentoring

Not every school or community has access to a mentor able to teach robotics skills. We pull volunteers from more experienced youth, educators, and professionals to mentor newer groups and their supervisors to lower the learning curve and get students tinkering sooner. We'll also host workshops to build skills, whether you're affiliated with a group or not.

Community

The community is one of the most special aspects of robotics. We aim to continue fostering this environment, connecting groups across regions so they can learn and grow together. We'll also have friendly competitions across regions.

What makes us different from other resources, you ask?

  • No administrative fees.

    We're entirely volunteer-run. All our funding goes straight to supporting youth.

  • You're not stuck here, but we won't ditch you either.

    We want groups to be able to function independently of us, but we also want to continue supporting them. Once they hit a steady pace, we let groups run on their own while we continue to support them through funding, outreach, and other opportunities. You're always part of the Quest Robotics Community.

  • It's not just a competition.

    We love competing in tournaments and challenges just as much as the next team. But here, competition is not the center. The core is learning, tinkering, and collaborating.
